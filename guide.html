Generic Sequencer Controls (Shared Architecture)

Global Div & Master Transport Global Bar

▶ Play / ■ Stop (Spacebar): Loops the sequence for live performance, transmitting real-time MIDI note events to connected hardware and triggering the internal browser audition synthesizer.

🔇 Mute (M): Toggles the browser Web Audio synthesizer auditioning on or off (starts muted to avoid unexpected output).

Safe: Off / On: Safety note rate limiter capping maximum note transmission to at most 8 notes per 500ms, protecting the M-VAVE FM-1 hardware microcontroller queue from buffer overflow.

Preset Slots (1–5): Fast local browser memory slots saving and recalling complete model parameters, seeds, and lock buffers.

Export / Import: Downloads or restores complete session states as reproducible, version-migrated .fm1p.json snapshot files.

Guide: Opens the dedicated in-app documentation modal for the currently active sequencer model.

Tempo (BPM): Master clock tempo (30–300 BPM). Automatically reads and snaps to FM-1 hardware BPM upon USB connection.

Speed: Autonomous simulation rate multiplier (0.1× to 4.0×) for continuous-time physics and population engines.

Length & Rate: Active pattern sequence length (1–64 steps) and clock division (1/1 down to 1/64, including dotted and triplet subdivisions).

Gate: Global note duration percentage relative to step duration.

Randomise (R / G): Re-rolls model-specific physical parameters and seeds without disturbing global transport or pitch settings.

Notes Div & Scale Quantization Notes Bar

Root & Octave: Sets the tonic pitch (C to B) and base octave (0 to 8) for scale quantization.

Scale Preset & Custom Grid: Choose from 20 musical scale presets (Major, Minor, Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian, Mixolydian, Pentatonics, Blues, Byzantine, Whole Tone, etc.) or construct custom micro-scales via the 12-semitone chromatic toggle strip.

Quantize: Snap intensity (0% raw pitch to 100% strict scale interval quantization).

Pitch Span: Number of octaves (1–4) spanned by the continuous mapping engine.

Octave Mode: Directional register expansion (Up, Down, Alternate, or Stochastic Random).

Polyphony Ceiling & Strategy: Strict hardware ceiling enforcing maximum concurrent voices (1–8) with automatic FIFO voice-stealing for M-VAVE FM-1 buffer stability. Features selectable voice allocation algorithms: Extrema, Lowest, Highest, Spread, Cluster, Cycle, and Random.

Sequence Div & Step Locking Sequence Bar

64-Step Pitch Matrix: Four 16-step bars with high-contrast live playhead ring indicators. Each cell displays its quantized note name and velocity opacity.

Direct Pitch Stepper: Select any step and use ‹ / › to nudge pitch by semitones.

Step Clear (✕): Empties a step of notes and locks it.

Step Locking (🔓 / 🔒): Freezes manual edits. Locked steps survive sequence regeneration, model switching, and randomisation, acting as compositional anchors.

Rhythm Div & 64-Step Gate Matrix Rhythm Bar

64-Step Gate Grid: Direct toggleable buttons for every step gate in the 64-step sequence.

Euclidean Generator: Algorithmic Euclidean hit/division engine with hit count, cycle steps, and rotation offset. Includes instant world rhythm presets (Tresillo 3:8, Cinquillo 5:8, Clave 3:2, Bossa Nova 5:16, Rumba 3:16, Shiko 4:16).

Rhythm Mute & Invert: Invert gate patterns or clear rhythmic tracks instantly.

Groove & Velocity Dynamics Groove Bar

Humanize: Introduces organic micro-timing jitter (0–100%) to prevent robotic quantization.

Swing: Delays even-numbered alternate steps with curated groove presets (Straight, Light 54%, MPC 58%, Hard 66%, Triplet 71%).

Velocity & Spread: Sets base MIDI note velocity (1–127) and random velocity variance.

Device Sound Design Tools

DX Patch Generator 6-OP FM Voice & Bank Design

Algorithmic sound designer and SysEx patch manager for Yamaha DX7 and FM-1 hardware. Generate patches across 11 sonic categories (Keys, Bass, Pads, Leads, Bells, Brass, Strings, Percussion, FX, Complex, Experimental). Features 6 sonic profiles, timbral macros, 6-operator envelope editing, internal 6-OP Web Audio FM preview, and 32-voice cartridge bank (4104B) export and upload.

Virtual Analogue (VA) Synthesizer Subtractive VA & 16-Preset Pack

Subtractive Virtual Analog sound design workbench tailored for the BaudGirl FM-1+VA custom firmware. Features 9 category recipes, 6 sonic profiles, interactive dual oscillator, filter, and ADSR envelope surfaces, real-time MIDI CC streaming, a 7-unit effects chain, and 16-preset VA pack management (slots 113–128).

Preset & Pattern Librarian Hardware Preset Export & Pattern Sync

Bidirectional hardware librarian and 64-step pattern workbench. Pull and archive all 128 presets from the FM-1 via SysEx 0x10 (exporting as 231B BaudGirl v4 .syx, 163B DX7 VCED, 4104B VMEM banks, or .json). Pull all 16 64-step patterns directly from device RAM via SysEx 0x11, edit polyphonic sequences on the interactive pitch/velocity canvas, or upload new patterns (SysEx 0x20) from .syx, .mid, .json, or Pulses banks.

Connectivity & Hardware Integration

USB & Bluetooth (BLE) MIDI

USB MIDI provides full two-way communication: pattern upload, hardware BPM synchronization, patch dumping, and real-time note broadcasting. Bluetooth (BLE) MIDI provides wireless live playback auditioning.

Strict 8-Voice Polyphony Ceiling

The M-VAVE FM-1 hardware sound engine is strictly limited to 8 voices. All polyphonic models in FM-1 Pulses enforce a voice allocation manager with FIFO note-stealing, ensuring zero dropped notes, CPU overruns, or hardware glitches.
